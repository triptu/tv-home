tv-home GitHub

Less, but better.

A home screen for Android TV and Google TV that shows a painting until you want to watch something.

Set it up GitHub

Art first.

A public-domain painting fills the screen and changes on its own. The clock, quote and dock stay small, and the dock fades when you stop pressing keys.

No ads, no tracking.

The launcher has no network access and no accounts. It lists the apps you installed and opens them.

Art mode and screensaver.

Art mode shows one painting at a time in a frame on a walnut wall, with a museum plaque. The screensaver is the same painting with its title, artist and year.

Debloat you can undo.

The TV's ads, recommendation rows, demos and telemetry are switched off, and the apps you don't use are removed. One script puts it all back. No root, no flashing.

Mac and phone remote.

A small Mac menu bar app drives the TV over adb, and a phone on the same Wi-Fi gets the same buttons from a page the Mac serves.

Set it up

  1. On the TV, turn on Developer options: in Settings, About, press OK on the build number seven times. Then turn on USB debugging and Wireless debugging.
  2. On a computer on the same Wi-Fi, give Claude Code or Codex this prompt:
    Set up github.com/triptu/tv-home on my TV. Follow PROMPT.md in that repo.
  3. Answer its questions about the apps you use, and accept "Allow debugging?" on the TV.

The README covers the rest: changing the dock, adding your own art, and undoing the setup.

Works on Android TV and Google TV with Android 8 or later. Not Samsung, LG or Fire TV.

A fork of tv-vibehack by Gathin23. The art is public domain or CC0, from museums and NASA; each picture's source is in the repo.
